# Insight Corruption audio production

This directory contains **configuration and provenance**, not release audio.

- `asset-registry.json` is the clearance gate for music/SFX.
- `episode-XX.production.json` is the editorial sound-design plan.
- Binary music, SFX, voice references and masters live in configured object/Modal storage unless the project explicitly chooses to version a small owned asset.

## Sound identity
The show should feel investigative, modern and restrained: low rhythmic pulse, sparse texture, clean chapter punctuation, and enough silence for consequential facts to land. Avoid true-crime cliché effects that imply guilt or manufacture drama.

A cue cannot ship while its registry status is `required-not-procured`. Procurement means the asset has a durable source, license/ownership record, creator/issuer, permitted uses, and checksum.
