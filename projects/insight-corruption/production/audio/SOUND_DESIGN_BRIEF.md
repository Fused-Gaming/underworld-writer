# Insight Corruption sound-design brief

## Identity
Modern investigative documentary, restrained rather than cinematic-horror. The signature should be recognizable within two seconds but leave room for Eric's voice.

## Assets to commission/procure
**Main theme:** 60-90 seconds plus stems and 15/30-second cutdowns. 80-96 BPM, sparse low percussion/pulse, restrained bass, subtle textural synth/organic element, clean ending button. No vocals.

**Investigation bed:** 2-4 minute seamless loop, minimal harmonic movement, no distracting lead melody, designed to sit roughly 20-26 dB beneath narration.

**Reflection bed:** 2-4 minute seamless loop, less rhythmic and slightly warmer/open; for known-vs-unknown and closing analysis.

**Chapter stinger:** 0.8-1.8 seconds, clean brand punctuation; no siren/gavel/gun/cash-register cliché.

**Midroll bumper:** 1.5-3 seconds, related to the theme but clearly separates editorial content from the house message.

Request 48 kHz/24-bit WAV masters and stems where applicable. Also request explicit perpetual commercial podcast/social/video rights or full ownership. Record every delivered file in `asset-registry.json` with SHA-256 and clearance metadata.

## Mix intent
Dialogue always wins. Beds enter/exit at editorial chapter boundaries, not every paragraph. Use silence intentionally after consequential facts. Stingers identify structure, not guilt or danger. The theme may return under the final CTA at reduced level.
