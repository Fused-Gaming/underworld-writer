## 🔍 Investigative Deep Dives

### Common Thread Analysis
All 5 cases reveal:
1. **Restitution as Prison Replacement** - Money buys lighter sentences
2. **Career Mitigation Strategy** - Long employment history excuses crimes
3. **Low-Level vs. Leadership** - Architects escape, participants face consequences
4. **Systemic Failures** - Detection takes years, sometimes decades
5. **Public Trust Erosion** - Government agencies revealed as corrupt

### Follow-Up Investigations
