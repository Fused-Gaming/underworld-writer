# 48 Felonies Against Four Cops: The Riders Scandal

*This article accompanies Episode 6 of* Insight Corruption*, a Bay Area government-corruption podcast that follows each case past the headline to the sentence.*

Officers Vasquez, Mabanag, Siapno, Hornung served as Oakland Police Officers (Narcotics Unit) in Oakland Police Department, as of 2000.

According to The Appeal - Police Corruption Files; Rolling Stone - Training Day Scandal; SF Chronicle - Oakland Police Timeline; Federal Oversight Reports, Officers Vasquez, Mabanag, Siapno, Hornung's offense breaks down like this: Four narcotics officers charged with 48 felonies including planting drugs, conducting false arrests, assaults, and systematic corruption in West Oakland. Known as the 'Riders Scandal'

## The Outcome
- **Outcome:** Most charges dismissed/not convicted
- **Status:** MINIMAL ACCOUNTABILITY DESPITE 48 FELONY CHARGES
**Fallout:** Hundreds of cases dismissed; Victims of false arrests never compensated; Federal consent decree imposed on OPD; Created mistrust in Oakland police; Set precedent for police corruption handling

## Why It Matters
Restitution used as a replacement for prison time
