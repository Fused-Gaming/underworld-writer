## Summary
Embezzled nearly $3.9 million from the Association of Bay Area Governments between 2011-2015, affecting affordable housing developments including Rincon Hill highrise and Windemere Ranch housing projects

## Key facts (from case file only)
- **Offense:** Embezzlement — Embezzled nearly $3.9 million from the Association of Bay Area Governments between 2011-2015, affecting affordable housing developments including Rincon Hill highrise and Windemere Ranch housing projects
- **Outcome:**
- **Prison Time:** 1 year and 1 day
- **Home Confinement:** 6 months
- **Probation:** 3 years supervised release
- **Restitution:** $3.5 million (paid by Howatt and family)
- **Status:** MINIMAL SENTENCE FOR MAJOR THEFT
- **Fallout:** Housing projects delayed for Bay Area residents; Public trust erosion in government financial management; Regional government oversight failures exposed; Families needing affordable housing suffered delays
- **How the outcome was reached:** Partial Restitution & Minimal Incarceration
