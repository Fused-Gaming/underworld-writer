# Finance Director Stole $3.9M from Bay Area Housing Fund, Got 1 Year in Prison

*This article accompanies Episode 2 of* Insight Corruption*, a Bay Area government-corruption podcast that follows each case past the headline to the sentence.*

Clarke Howatt served as Finance Director, Association of Bay Area Governments (ABAG) in Bay Area Regional Government, as of 2016.

According to Palo Alto Online - 2016; Bay Area News Group; Federal Court Records, Clarke Howatt's offense breaks down like this: Embezzled nearly $3.9 million from the Association of Bay Area Governments between 2011-2015, affecting affordable housing developments including Rincon Hill highrise and Windemere Ranch housing projects

## The Outcome
- **Prison Time:** 1 year and 1 day
- **Home Confinement:** 6 months
- **Probation:** 3 years supervised release
- **Restitution:** $3.5 million (paid by Howatt and family)
- **Status:** MINIMAL SENTENCE FOR MAJOR THEFT
**Fallout:** Housing projects delayed for Bay Area residents; Public trust erosion in government financial management; Regional government oversight failures exposed; Families needing affordable housing suffered delays

Public service records cited to minimize sentences for federal crimes
