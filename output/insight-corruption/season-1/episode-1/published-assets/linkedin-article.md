# Union Boss Smuggled 17,000 Opioid Pills, Faced Zero Jail Time

*This article accompanies Episode 1 of* Insight Corruption*, a Bay Area government-corruption podcast that follows each case past the headline to the sentence.*

Joanne Segovia served as Executive Director, San Jose Police Officers' Association in San Jose, California, as of 2024.

According to San José Spotlight - 2024; NBC Bay Area - 2024; KQED News - 2024, Joanne Segovia's offense breaks down like this: Smuggled over 17,000 opioid pills (Tapentadol) into the U.S. from India over multiple years while serving as head of the police union

## The Outcome
- **Prison Time:** Zero days
- **Probation:** 3 years
- **Community Service:** 100 hours
- **Fines:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Status:** AVOIDED INCARCERATION
**Fallout:** Loss of union position; Professional disgrace in law enforcement community; Permanent criminal record; Public trust erosion in police union

## Why It Matters
Restitution used as a replacement for prison time
