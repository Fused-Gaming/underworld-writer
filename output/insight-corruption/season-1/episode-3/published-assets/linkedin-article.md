# SF Building Inspector Took Bribes for 14 Years, Served Only 1 Year in Prison

*This article accompanies Episode 3 of* Insight Corruption*, a Bay Area government-corruption podcast that follows each case past the headline to the sentence.*

Rodolfo Pada served as Building Inspector, San Francisco Department of Building Inspection in San Francisco, California, as of 2026.

According to SF Chronicle - 2026; Federal Court Records; Department of Building Inspection Records, Rodolfo Pada's offense breaks down like this: Accepted over $40,000 in bribes from construction firms over 14 years (2003-2017) to approve permits, expedite inspections, and approve building plans - directly compromising building code enforcement and public safety

## The Outcome
- **Prison Time:** 1 year and 1 day
- **Probation:** Supervised release
- **Restitution:** Repayment of bribe amounts
- **Status:** MINIMAL SENTENCE FOR 14-YEAR BRIBERY SCHEME
**Fallout:** Loss of career with public pension implications; Buildings approved without proper safety compliance; Public trust erosion in building safety oversight; Questions about which buildings were approved fraudulently
