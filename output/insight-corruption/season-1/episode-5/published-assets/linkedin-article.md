*This article accompanies Episode 5 of* Insight Corruption*, a Bay Area government-corruption podcast that follows each case past the headline to the sentence.*

Florence Kong served as Contractor in San Francisco, California, as of 2023.

- **Prison Time:** 1 year and 1 day

- **Status:** LIGHT SENTENCE FOR LARGE BRIBERY SCHEME
**Fallout:** Loss of city contract opportunities; Construction projects awarded through corruption exposed; Contractor licensing questions; Contract-award integrity compromised
