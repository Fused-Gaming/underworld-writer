# Producer Brief: Episode 15 — The Docket Watch

**Series:** Insight Corruption
**Duration target:** 30 minutes + one 90-second mid-roll sponsor break

## Why this episode exists
Ten of the original 24 compiled case files (oakland-cases/sheng-thao-case.json, oakland-cases/phong-tran-case.json, oakland-cases/kevin-arias-case.json, oakland-cases/helicopter-pilots-case.json, oakland-cases/ben-chavis-case.json, oakland-cases/alameda-county-assessor-fraud.json, oakland-cases/alameda-probation-officer-fraud.json, oakland-cases/alameda-sheriff-corruption.json, oakland-cases/oakland-public-works-official.json, oakland-cases/transit-authority-fraud.json) describe
cases that are pending, under investigation, or awaiting sentencing. None fit the
season's "how they got away with it" premise, because none has a final outcome.
Rather than force-fitting them into that frame — or quietly dropping them —
this episode gives them an honest, clearly-labeled format of their own.

## Pre-air checklist
- [ ] Re-check PACER/court dockets for each of the 10 cases; several are likely
      to have moved since the case files were compiled.
- [ ] Confirm presumption-of-innocence language is retained for every case
      without a plea or verdict.
- [ ] If any case has resolved by air date, move it to a full episode of its
