# The Docket Watch: 10 Bay Area Corruption Cases Still Moving Through Court

*This article accompanies the Season 1 finale of* Insight Corruption. *Unlike Episodes 1-14, none of the cases below have a resolved outcome, so none get this show's usual "how they got away with it" framing.*

### Sheng Thao
**Status:** PENDING - Case in Federal Court. Presumption of innocence applies.

Indicted January 2025 on federal charges along with 3 co-defendants (Duong family and associates) for a bribery and corruption scheme involving city contracts worth millions. Scheme allegedly involved exchanging official city actions for personal benefits.

*Sources: KQED - Oakland Corruption Coverage; OaklandSide - FBI Indictment Analysis; Department of Justice - Official Indictment; SF Chronicle - Oakland Corruption Timeline; NBC News Bay Area - 2025*

### Phong Tran
**Status:** CASE IN PROGRESS. Presumption of innocence applies.

Detective charged with perjury and witness bribery affecting over 125+ cases. Systematic corruption in criminal cases undermining justice system.

*Sources: KTVU - OPD Investigation; NBC Bay Area - Detective Corruption; SF Chronicle - Oakland Police Misconduct*

### Kevin Arias
**Status:** CASE IN PROGRESS. Presumption of innocence applies.

Sergeant charged with falsifying timesheets to fraudulently claim $38,000 in overtime pay

*Sources: KTVU - OPD Investigation; SF Chronicle - Oakland Police Misconduct*

### Brandon Mart & David Mac
**Status:** CASE IN PROGRESS. Presumption of innocence applies.

Two helicopter pilots charged with conspiracy to commit FAA fraud involving false certifications and licensing violations

*Sources: Federal Aviation Administration; SF Chronicle - Oakland Police Misconduct*

### Ben Chavis
**Status:** CASE UNDER INVESTIGATION/PROSECUTION. Presumption of innocence applies.

Embezzled $3.7M+ in school grants and funds by funneling money to personal companies and businesses under shell entities

*Sources: SF Chronicle - Education Fraud Investigation; KQED - Oakland Schools Corruption*

### Assessor's Office Official
**Status:** CASE UNDER PROSECUTION. Presumption of innocence applies.

Official accepted bribes to fraudulently lower property assessments for select clients, reducing tax liability

*Sources: Alameda County District Attorney; SF Chronicle*

### Probation Officer

### Sheriff Deputy
**Status:** CASE PENDING. Presumption of innocence applies.

### Public Works Official
**Status:** CASE UNDER INVESTIGATION. Presumption of innocence applies.
