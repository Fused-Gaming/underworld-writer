# Guest Handoff: Guest Script: The SolarWinds Operators

**Episode Format:** Two Parts
**Expected Runtime:** 3 minutes per part

## Background Information
Highly sophisticated APT group with military-grade capabilities, focused on espionage and long-term network access. Known for sophisticated supply chain attacks and methodical operational security.

## Discussion Topics
- The vulnerabilities of software supply chains
- Nation-state cyber warfare and espionage
- The asymmetry between defenders and sophisticated attackers
- Unintended consequences of interconnected infrastructure
- The shadow world of state-sponsored cyber operations
- Trust and transparency in technology
