# Producer Brief: The Story of The SolarWinds Operators

**Character:** The SolarWinds Operators
**Duration:** 14-20 minutes (2 parts)

## Episode Summary
Russian state-sponsored intelligence agency (SVR) operating in cyberspace since the late 2000s

## Key Facts
- Highly sophisticated APT group with military-grade capabilities, focused on espionage and long-term network access. Known for sophisticated supply chain attacks and methodical operational security.
- Role: State-Sponsored APT Team Leader / SVR Cyber Operations Division
- Faction: Russian Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR)
- December 2020 onwards: FireEye discovered the breach, U.S. government response, attribution to SVR confirmed, international sanctions imposed, operation exposed but legacy compromises persist
