# The Homeless Lunchbox

Season 1 — 10 episodes, single-part format, 15 minutes + one 30-second sponsor
break per episode. Two cohosts eat lunch from a different real Bay Area
homeless-services organization each episode and discuss a related policy topic.

| Ep | Organization | Location | Topic |
|----|--------------|----------|-------|
| 1 | GLIDE | San Francisco (Tenderloin) | Encampment sweeps and sidewalk-clearing enforcement |
| 2 | St. Anthony's Foundation Dining Room | San Francisco (Tenderloin) | Prop 36 and drug-crisis policy in the Tenderloin |
| 3 | St. Mary's Center | Oakland | Aging into homelessness and encampment closures in Oakland |
| 4 | Bay Area Rescue Mission | Richmond | CARE Court in Contra Costa County |
| 5 | Trinity Center | Walnut Creek | Suburban homelessness and shelter-siting opposition |
| 6 | Sacred Heart Community Service | San Jose | Housing First policy debate in Santa Clara County |
| 7 | Martha's Kitchen | San Jose | CalFresh access barriers for unhoused residents |
| 8 | LifeMoves | San Mateo / Santa Clara counties (Peninsula) | Tech-sector philanthropy in Silicon Valley homelessness response |
| 9 | Ecumenical Hunger Program | East Palo Alto | Displacement pressure in East Palo Alto |
| 10 | COTS (Committee on the Shelterless) | Novato (Marin County) | Marin County's housing targets and shelter-bed capacity |

**Before producing any episode:** contact the featured organization for
permission and context. The meal-tasting and any time-sensitive policy claims
are deliberately left as placeholders in each script - see producer-briefs/.
