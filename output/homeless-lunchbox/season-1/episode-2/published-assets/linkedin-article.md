## The Bay Area Beat: Prop 36 and drug-crisis policy in the Tenderloin
California voters passed Proposition 36 in November 2024, increasing penalties for certain drug and theft offenses and partially rolling back Prop 47 (2014); advocates and critics disagree sharply on its effect on unhoused Californians.
