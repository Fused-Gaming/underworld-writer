## The Bay Area Beat: Marin County's housing targets and shelter-bed capacity
California's Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA) process sets state-mandated housing-production targets for each county and city, and Marin's low-density zoning history is frequently cited in coverage of the county's housing shortfall.
